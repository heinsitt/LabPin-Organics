# 🌿 LabPin Organics

&gt; Fresh organic vegetables from Kyaukphyu, Rakhine State. Grown with care, delivered with pride.

![LabPin Organics](images/logo.jpg)

## 📌 About

**LabPin Organics** is a local mini organic fruits & vegetables business website built to showcase fresh produce directly from the farm. The project highlights our commitment to natural farming, quality harvests, and community-driven agriculture in Rakhine State, Myanmar.
