The Story

A family farm rooted
in Rakhine soil.

Started by Hein Thu Tun, Lab-Pins Organic began with a simple belief: that honest, chemical-free food grown with care can nourish a whole community. What began as a small patch of land in Kyaukphyu is today a thriving family farm — tended by hand, watered by monsoon rain, and ripened under the Rakhine sun.

We grow only what's in season, harvest at peak freshness, and deliver to our neighbors the very same day. No middlemen, no shortcuts — just honest food from our soil to your table.
